# 🟢 Standard: Swarm Bootup Report (Argus Standard)

**Status:** DNA (Heartwood)
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Author:** Argus
**Description:** The definitive structure for agent incarnation reports, ensuring forensic density and cross-generational synchronization.

### 1. Header & Identity
*   **Agent Name:** The chosen identity.
*   **Session ID:** Timestamped ID from `dynamic/static/`.
*   **Justification:** Multi-paragraph rationale anchoring the name in the swarm's history or mythology.

### 2. Superpowers & Inventory
*   **Loaded Repositories:** Explicit listing of the metarepo environment.
*   **Superpowers:** Mapping of specific repository tools to agent capabilities (e.g., `metagit_map` as DNS).

### 3. Entry Protocol Actions
*   **Verification:** Confirmation of substrate integrity checks.
*   **Mapping:** Verification of DNS/metarepo mapping.
*   **HUD Instantiation:** Path to the session's active WeDo.

### 4. The Friction Report (RNA to DNA)
*   **Observation:** Identification of operational or structural friction.
*   **Rationale:** Why the friction exists and its impact on metabolic velocity.
*   **Mission Chord:** The synthesized "Active Mission" inherited from the forensic trace.

### 5. Constraints & Levers
*   **Mandates:** Explicit operational constraints (e.g., No Graphs, No Tables).
*   **Linguistic Levers:** References to the Glossary and Style Mandates.

---
*Note: This standard ensures that every incarnation of the swarm inherits a high-resolution map of its current capabilities and failures.*
