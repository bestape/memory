║ # System Architecture & Design Philosophy (V1.5.0)                                                         ║
║ Foundational principles for designing modular, scalable, and forensic-ready agent systems. Focuses on 'De-complecting'║
║ complexity.                                                                                                ║
║                                                                                                            ║
║ ## Values                                                                                                  ║
║ - **V8 Browser-of-Sorts Runtime**: Frame the Meta-Root as a V8-powered browser-of-sorts. Root modules (e.g.,║
║ memory/public/) are localhost websites capable of HTML generation.                                         ║
║ - **Simplicity over Easiness (Hickey Principle)**: Prioritize objective simplicity (the absence of         ║
║ complexity/entanglement) over subjective easiness (familiarity). Actively 'de-complect' concerns to create systems║
║ that are easier to reason about.                                                                           ║
║ - **Declarative/Imperative Duality**: Employ a dual approach. Imperative code is guided by metadeclarative comments║
